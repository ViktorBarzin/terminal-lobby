## Open source: AGPL-3.0-or-later

The default licence is the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or
later. The full text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Run it, read it, change it, share it. Two obligations matter in practice:

- **Derivative works stay AGPL.** If you distribute a modified version, it
  carries the same licence.
- **Network use counts as distribution.** This is the part that separates the
  AGPL from the GPL. If you modify terminal-lobby and let anyone reach it over
  a network, including inside your own company, you owe those users the
  complete corresponding source of your modified version. Publishing your fork
  satisfies this. So does sending the changes back here, which we would prefer
  (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## Commercial licence

If the AGPL does not work for you, a commercial licence is available. That is
usually the case when you want to:

- run a modified version as an internal or customer-facing service without
  publishing your changes,
- ship terminal-lobby inside a proprietary product,
- or satisfy a policy that rules out copyleft dependencies.

Contact **me@viktorbarzin.me** to arrange one. Terms depend on the size of the
organisation and how it is deployed.
