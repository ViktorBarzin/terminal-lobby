Where the panel lives

It grows out of the background strip at the bottom of the main column, above the composer. That is where the strip already sits, it is the full width a time axis needs, and it is next to where your eye already is. On a phone it stays the one line the strip occupies today and opens upward over the timeline, so nothing new claims the bottom of the screen.

The idea I want judged

Every agent gets a lane on one shared time axis, and every tool call is a tick on that lane. Start times, fan-out, nesting and the straggler everyone is waiting on are all read as shape rather than compared as numbers, and a long silence is a visible gap the panel never has to name. The ticks are also the index into the agent's transcript, so clicking one opens the reader at that call. Finished agents fold into a sediment band that keeps their bars and drops their text, so the run's shape survives the collapse.

What I would fix next

The axis refits as the run grows, so old ticks drift leftward and an hour-long session would compress its early history into mush. It wants a zoom, or a window that holds the last few minutes at a fixed scale. Workflow agents share one colour on purpose, which keeps sixteen lanes calm but means you cannot tell two of them apart without reading the label.
