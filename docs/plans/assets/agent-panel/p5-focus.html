Where it lives. A sticky band at the top of the main column, under the session bar, on the timeline's own 860px measure. The question it answers, is anything wrong, gets asked before you read the conversation, so it sits where you look first and it does not scroll away. It also stays clear of the composer and the phone keyboard. The old bottom strip is gone rather than duplicated; being at the bottom is part of why nobody read it.

The idea I want judged. One agent is promoted to a lead card that spells out its current tool call with arguments, its numbers, and a trail of its last four calls. The other agents stay a one line index in spawn order, which never re-sorts under your cursor. The lead is chosen by stated facts in a fixed order, error first, then longest quiet, then most tokens, and the card says which one applied. If the rule picks wrong you click any row to pin your own choice, and Focus / List switches to an even list in one move. So the panel commits to a choice, shows its reasoning, and is cheap to overrule.

What it refuses to say. Silence is only ever a number, quiet 3m 12s and last record 3m 12s ago. There is no progress bar, no health, no judgement. Everything drawn here exists in the measured data.

What I would fix with more time. Token burn is a weak proxy for which running agent matters when sixteen are healthy and none is quiet, so the lead drifts to whichever model happened to write more. I would rank on first error inside a phase, let you lock focus to one workflow phase, and let the lead card keep its own small scroll of the agent's last messages rather than only tool names.
